# Franchise Development Fee & Territory Reconciliation

Recover franchise development fees and enforce territory and unit-opening obligations across development schedules.

**Primary buyer:** Franchisors and multi-brand operators. **Evidence:** development agreements, territories, unit schedules, site approvals, opening dates, initial fees, renewal fees, transfer fees, defaults, invoices, and payments.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Development agreement library
- Territory boundary mapping
- Franchisee entity registry
- Unit schedule tracking
- Site approval milestones
- Opening deadline control
- Initial fee calculation
- Renewal fee calculation
- Transfer fee calculation
- Development default detection
- Notice cure workflow
- Invoice generation
- Franchisee dispute workflow
- Cash reconciliation
- Territory unit analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4734>. API: `5734`.
